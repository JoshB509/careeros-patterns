# VSL shot list · Executive Communication Program

The full VSL script v02 with the shot for every part. Slides are `10_Sales/VSL/2026-10_executive-communication-program_vsl_slides_v01.html`.

Record the slides as one pass. Each animation starts on a grey card with its number, so you can find it on the timeline and cut it in where the shot list says. Space or the right arrow moves on a beat, left goes back, H shows the beat counter, F goes full screen, and typing a number then Enter jumps to that beat. After the last press of an animation, the next press brings up the next number card.

Picture-in-picture animations leave the same block on the right free every time (1322 px in, 202 px down, 518 by 674 px on a 1920 by 1080 frame), which is the box from your PowerPoint layout.
